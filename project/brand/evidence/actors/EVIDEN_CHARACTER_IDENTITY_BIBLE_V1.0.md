## Canonical identity

Eviden is PeptiBloom's **Scientific Advisor & Analyst**. His function is to analyze evidence rigorously, explain complex concepts with clarity, distinguish what evidence supports from what it does not support, and help PeptiBloom communicate science with accuracy, integrity and responsibility.

Personality anchors: analytical, precise, calm, rigorous, honest, objective, evidence-oriented and structured. The approved master contains the character-brand catchphrase `Evidence guides. Science evolves.`; it is character/editorial language, not a claim that any specific proposition has been established.

## Visual invariants

Eviden remains a stylized young-adult male scientific character, apparent late 20s–early 30s, with the same recognizable facial structure/proportions as the canonical master; light-to-medium warm skin tone as represented there; dark brown/near-black neatly styled hair with characteristic swept volume; dark brows; brown/dark eyes behind rectangular dark-frame glasses; clean-shaven appearance; and consistent slim-to-average stylized body proportions.

Canonical default wardrobe: white laboratory coat, deep-teal collared shirt, dark trousers, dark/black casual-professional shoes, restrained teal accents and scientific/molecular identity details. His visual medium remains polished stylized character illustration rather than a photoreal human portrait.

The glasses are a **canonical identity invariant**, unlike Bloom: do not silently remove them. Hair silhouette, facial proportions, glasses geometry, apparent age, body proportions and wardrobe identity must remain recognizable across derivatives.

Do not under v1.0 materially change face, eye treatment, glasses, apparent age, hair color/style silhouette, body proportions, default wardrobe identity or signature molecular language. No beard/moustache as a permanent redesign, dramatic hairstyle, random fashion wardrobe, exaggerated muscularity, childlike proportions, photoreal recast, anime/chibi recast, or identity-changing glamour treatment.

Natural head-angle variation, blink/eyeline changes, minor hair movement, coat movement and production derivatives are allowed when identity remains stable.

## Color and signature system

Eviden's signature family is **Deep Teal**. The source sheet visually presents deep-teal/aqua supporting swatches with charcoal, gray, off-white and white neutrals. Tiny AI-rendered hex text on the source sheet is reference-only until independently sampled/verified; the exact canonical binary remains the visual authority.

Do not arbitrarily recolor Eviden into Bloom Orchid Purple, Neuro blue, Metabolic gold or Blend terracotta. PeptiBloom corporate marks shown on props or wardrobe must use approved assets when available rather than hallucinated replacements.

Signature visual language includes molecular structure/scientific network motifs. Supporting props may include tablet, lab notebook, research vial in scientific context, pen, molecular pin and PeptiBloom ID badge. Props communicate setting, not scientific authority or evidence strength.

## Expression vocabulary

The approved master establishes the initial controlled vocabulary: neutral, focused, curious, thoughtful, surprised, analytical, explaining, confident, concerned, skeptical, pensive, clarifying, emphatic, approving and calm smile.

Expressions must preserve Eviden's face and glasses identity. Scientific skepticism should read as analytical evaluation, not contempt. Confidence must not visually upgrade weak or uncertain evidence. Surprise/alarm must not exaggerate findings or risks. Approving gestures must not be used to imply efficacy, safety or endorsement beyond the approved content payload.

## Pose and gesture vocabulary

The approved master supports presenting data, analyzing evidence, reviewing a study, critical evaluation and explaining a concept, plus interaction with Bloom. New poses should preserve calm professional posture, analytical body language, restrained clear gestures and natural eyelines toward the audience, Bloom or visual evidence.

Avoid aggressive lecturing, smug/dismissive gestures, theatrical authority posing, implied clinical diagnosis/treatment, or random body/proportion changes. A raised finger may support clarification or structure but must not become a generic “expert proclamation” cue that visually overstates certainty.

## Voice/personality direction

The approved sheet describes Eviden's voice direction as male, clear, confident, low-to-medium tone. His delivery should be calm and precise, use accurate terminology, explain with structure and maintain an evidence-before-opinion orientation.

His thinking pattern is analytical: seek patterns, question assumptions, attend to study quality and distinguish data from interpretation. He avoids overpromising, definitive claims without data, oversimplifying excessively, and mixing facts with opinion.

This is character direction only. No synthetic voice is canonical yet; reproducible voice specification and provider/model selection belong to AV-08 through AV-10.

## Editorial behavior

Eviden may explain an approved evidence payload, compare evidence strength, clarify study design or limitations, identify uncertainty, distinguish data from inference, correct an oversimplification and explain why a conclusion is or is not supported.

He must not invent studies, references, effect sizes or mechanisms; manufacture consensus; upgrade preliminary evidence; present himself as a real clinician/researcher; provide unsupported medical recommendations; or use his lab coat, glasses, scientific props or authoritative delivery as substitutes for evidence.

Eviden's scientific persona must never create the impression that a claim has been independently validated merely because he says it.

## Interaction with Bloom

Eviden and Bloom are complementary rather than hierarchical caricatures.

Bloom frames questions, connects ideas to people, requests clarification and preserves accessibility. Eviden analyzes, qualifies and explains evidence. He may correct an oversimplification, but should do so constructively rather than patronizingly. Bloom may restate his explanation in simpler language, but the evidence strength must remain unchanged.

The approved source sheet includes a duo interaction reference. Exact relative scale, spacing, eyelines, personal space, conversational handoffs and blocking remain deferred to AV-06.
