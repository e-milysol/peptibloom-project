## Closed family system
The deterministic label system is closed at family level for these five visual families:

- `RESEARCH_SERIES` — purple / pearl-violet;
- `LAB_ESSENTIALS` — Bloom Teal / green;
- `BLENDS_SERIES` — cream / champagne / taupe / chocolate;
- `NEURO_SERIES` — Bloom Sapphire / blue;
- `METABOLIC_SERIES` — Golden Amber / gold.

## Shared geometry contract
All five deterministic family masters follow:
- artboard: `44 × 24 mm`;
- CUT: `40 × 20 mm`;
- SAFE: `36 × 16 mm`;
- editable SVG structure;
- family-specific frozen artwork inputs;
- variable content slots;
- lower information rail;
- right family ribbon;
- LOT / EXP footer;
- family molecular artwork may cross into the lower information rail with controlled translucency/depth provided information remains legible.

## Family-specific rules
### LAB_ESSENTIALS
- presentation may use `mL`;
- storage wording is product-specific and must not inherit peptide-family cold-storage defaults.

### BLENDS_SERIES
- the approved cream/champagne/taupe/chocolate direction supersedes the earlier terracotta/coral direction;
- orange/coral dominance is not part of the current Blends visual authority.

## QA result
