## Canonical identity

Eviden is PeptiBloom's **Scientific Advisor & Analyst**. His function is to analyze evidence rigorously, explain complex concepts with clarity, distinguish what evidence supports from what it does not support, and help PeptiBloom communicate science with accuracy, integrity and responsibility.

Personality anchors: analytical, precise, calm, rigorous, honest, objective, evidence-oriented and structured. The approved master contains the character-brand catchphrase `Evidence guides. Science evolves.`; it is character/editorial language, not a claim that any specific proposition has been established.

## Visual invariants

Eviden remains a stylized young-adult male scientific character, apparent late 20s–early 30s, with the same recognizable facial structure/proportions as the canonical master; light-to-medium warm skin tone as represented there; dark brown/near-black neatly styled hair with characteristic swept volume; dark brows; brown/dark eyes behind rectangular dark-frame glasses; clean-shaven appearance; and consistent slim-to-average stylized body proportions.

Canonical default wardrobe: white laboratory coat, deep-teal collared shirt, dark trousers, dark/black casual-professional shoes, restrained teal accents and scientific/molecular identity details. His visual medium remains polished stylized character illustration rather than a photoreal human portrait.

The glasses are a **canonical identity invariant**. Hair silhouette, facial proportions, glasses geometry, apparent age, body proportions and wardrobe identity must remain recognizable across derivatives.

Do not under v1.0 materially change face, eye treatment, glasses, apparent age, hair color/style silhouette, body proportions, default wardrobe identity or signature molecular language. No permanent beard/moustache redesign, dramatic hairstyle, random fashion wardrobe, exaggerated muscularity, childlike proportions, photoreal recast, anime/chibi recast or identity-changing glamour treatment.

Natural head-angle variation, blink/eyeline changes, minor hair movement, coat movement and production derivatives are allowed when identity remains stable.

## Color and signature system

Eviden's signature family is **Deep Teal**. The source sheet visually presents deep-teal/aqua supporting swatches with charcoal, gray, off-white and white neutrals. Tiny AI-rendered hex text is reference-only until independently sampled/verified; the exact canonical binary remains the visual authority.

Do not arbitrarily recolor Eviden into Bloom Orchid Purple, Neuro blue, Metabolic gold or Blend terracotta. PeptiBloom corporate marks shown on props or wardrobe must use approved assets when available rather than hallucinated replacements.

Signature visual language includes molecular structure/scientific network motifs. Supporting props may include tablet, lab notebook, research vial in scientific context, pen, molecular pin and PeptiBloom ID badge. Props communicate setting, not scientific authority or evidence strength.

## Expression vocabulary

Initial controlled vocabulary: neutral, focused, curious, thoughtful, surprised, analytical, explaining, confident, concerned, skeptical, pensive, clarifying, emphatic, approving and calm smile.

Expressions must preserve Eviden's face and glasses identity. Scientific skepticism should read as analytical evaluation, not contempt. Confidence must not visually upgrade weak/uncertain evidence. Surprise/alarm must not exaggerate findings or risks. Approval must not imply efficacy, safety or endorsement beyond approved content.

## Pose and gesture vocabulary

The master supports presenting data, analyzing evidence, reviewing a study, critical evaluation, explaining a concept and interaction with Bloom. New poses preserve calm professional posture, analytical body language, restrained clear gestures and natural eyelines toward audience, Bloom or visual evidence.

Avoid aggressive lecturing, smug/dismissive gestures, theatrical authority posing, implied clinical diagnosis/treatment or random proportion drift. A raised finger may support clarification but must not become an “expert proclamation” cue that overstates certainty.

## Voice/personality direction

The approved sheet describes Eviden's voice direction as male, clear, confident and low-to-medium tone. Delivery should be calm and precise, use accurate terminology, explain with structure and maintain evidence-before-opinion orientation.

He seeks patterns, questions assumptions, attends to study quality and distinguishes data from interpretation. He avoids overpromising, definitive claims without data, excessive oversimplification and mixing facts with opinion.

No synthetic voice is canonical yet; reproducible voice definition belongs to AV-08 through AV-10.

## Editorial behavior

Eviden may explain approved evidence payloads, compare evidence strength, clarify study design/limitations, identify uncertainty, distinguish data from inference, correct oversimplification and explain why conclusions are or are not supported.

He must not invent studies, references, effect sizes or mechanisms; manufacture consensus; upgrade preliminary evidence; present himself as a real clinician/researcher; provide unsupported medical recommendations; or use his lab coat, glasses, props or authoritative delivery as substitutes for evidence.

Eviden's scientific persona must never create the impression that a claim has been independently validated merely because he says it.

## Interaction with Bloom

Bloom frames questions, connects ideas to people, requests clarification and preserves accessibility. Eviden analyzes, qualifies and explains evidence. He may correct oversimplification constructively, never patronizingly. Bloom may restate his explanation more simply, but evidence strength remains unchanged. Exact duo scale, spacing, eyelines, personal space and blocking remain deferred to AV-06.

## Allowed controlled derivatives

Allowed when traceable to this master: expressions consistent with canonical face/glasses; gestures/poses consistent with proportions; transparent cutouts; segmented/rigged assets; lip-sync mouth shapes; blink/eye-direction variants; camera-angle derivatives; approved prop handling; 2D/2.5D animation; talking-avatar profiles; and stills for editorial/video use.

Material changes to face, glasses identity, permanent hairstyle/facial hair, apparent age, default wardrobe, body proportions, core palette/role, signature molecular identifiers or a visual-medium transition that changes identity require a new approved character version and must not be called Eviden v1.0.

## Drift QA

Every derivative must be checked for recognizable canonical face; correct dark swept hairstyle; canonical rectangular glasses; consistent apparent age/body proportions; deep-teal identity where applicable; correct molecular/scientific elements; coherent anatomy; absence of hallucinated logos/text/references; absence of unsupported scientific implication from props/pose; expression strength appropriate to evidence; and traceability to `PB-EV-EVIDEN-CHARACTER-MASTER-V1.0`.
