# PB-XD-001 — Public catalog label legal/regulatory review

FROM: PACKAGING / LABELS
TO: LEGAL / REGULATORY + WEB / CATALOG
TYPE: CROSS-DOMAIN
STATUS: OPEN
CREATED: 2026-09-03

## Need
Pre-publication Legal/Regulatory review of a proposed public-catalog visual system in which each research material is represented primarily by a PeptiBloom family-coded label/card rather than by a conventional ecommerce/product-sales card.

The proposed visual family currently has five concept variants:
- Neuro — blue / Bloom Sapphire — example: SELANK 10 mg;
- regenerative/research family — green/teal — example: GHK-Cu 50 mg;
- Metabolic — gold — example: RETATRUTIDE 10 mg;
- longevity/research family — purple — example: EPITHALON 10 mg;
- terracotta family — example concept: PT-141 10 mg.

These are CONCEPT / REVIEW assets only. They are not approved packaging, scientific canon, legal labeling, public catalog authorization, or READY_FOR_SALE evidence.

## Proposed public use
Potential architecture for review:

`PUBLIC CATALOG -> family-coded label/card -> neutral technical material information / request-information surface`

The label/card would be the principal visual representation of the material. The proposal deliberately avoids treating the Scientific Library as a sales landing page. Scientific Library and commercial/catalog surfaces remain structurally distinguishable in accordance with current PeptiBloom canon.

No publication, implementation, product-route creation or sales-flow change is authorized by this request.

## Elements requiring explicit review
Please assess the public-facing acceptability and required wording/evidence for each element independently and in combination:

1. compound name on the card (e.g. Selank, GHK-Cu, Retatrutide, Epithalon, PT-141);
2. presentation/quantity in `mg`;
3. `RESEARCH USE ONLY`;
4. `LABORATORY USE ONLY`;
5. `HIGH PURITY RESEARCH` or any purity/quality wording;
6. `STORE COLD 2–8 °C` or any storage statement;
7. family/series naming and associated iconography, including whether terms such as `NEURO`, `METABOLIC`, `LONGEVITY`, `REGENERATIVE` or `PERFORMANCE` could themselves create an impermissible or misleading implied human-use/benefit claim;
8. descriptors such as `NEUROPEPTIDE RESEARCH REFERENCE`, `METABOLIC PEPTIDE RESEARCH REFERENCE`, `BIOACTIVE PEPTIDE RESEARCH REFERENCE`, etc.;
9. LOT and EXP fields;
10. QR code and `peptibloomproject.com` destination;
11. PeptiBloom branding and `SCIENCE · PURITY · BLOOM` when displayed on the same visual;
12. the combined visual impression of a polished pharmaceutical-style label/card even when RUO/laboratory-only language is present;
13. whether a neutral CTA such as `Request information` is permissible and, if so, what restrictions must apply to the destination/contact flow;
14. whether linking or cross-linking the catalog card to the independent Scientific Library would materially change the legal/regulatory assessment.

## Question / decision required
For Spain/EU public web publication, determine one of the following for this proposed visual/catalog model:

- `APPROVABLE WITH CONDITIONS` — identify exact mandatory wording, removals, evidence requirements, destination/CTA restrictions and compound/category exclusions;
- `HOLD / NOT APPROVABLE AS PROPOSED` — identify the blocking elements and whether a materially different non-promotional technical presentation could be reviewed;
- `PRODUCT-BY-PRODUCT REVIEW REQUIRED` — identify which legal/regulatory classification facts must be established before any individual compound can be displayed publicly.

Please explicitly state whether `RUO` / `Laboratory Use Only` wording changes the assessment or merely forms one factor in the overall presentation.

## Evidence / substantiation gate
Packaging must not turn supplier statements or generic template text into PeptiBloom claims. Before any final asset is produced or published:

- purity/quality wording must have an approved evidentiary basis appropriate to the claim and, where relevant, the actual batch/lot;
- storage conditions must be supported for the exact material/form rather than inherited globally from the template;
- LOT/EXP semantics and any expiry date must have a defensible operational/quality basis;
- scientific descriptors must pass the applicable Evidence/Scientific Inventory process if they constitute scientific claims;
- a QR must resolve to a real maintained and approved destination;
- product identity/presentation must come from approved Catalog/Product truth rather than from the mockup itself.

## Brand/design note
The visual concept uses PeptiBloom orchid + DNA + molecular motifs and family colors. Final production artwork must reuse the canonical PeptiBloom logo or an approved derivative. AI/mockup-rendered approximations of the logo are not acceptable as production brand assets.

## Constraints
- `PB-XD request != approval != shared canon`.
- Do not infer legal clearance from `Research Use Only` or `Laboratory Use Only`.
- Do not infer human-use suitability, medicinal authorization, clinical-trial product suitability, efficacy, safety, sterility, potency or purity from the visual.
- Do not infer READY_FOR_SALE or publication authorization.
- Do not add dosing, reconstitution, route, administration, cycle or human-use instructions to the catalog surface as part of this review.
- Science and commerce must remain structurally distinguishable.
- Compound/public scientific pages remain Evidence-gated under current canon.
- No current public implementation should proceed until the applicable Legal/Regulatory, Evidence/claim, Catalog/product-truth, Brand and Owner/Web publication gates are satisfied.

## Source concepts / review artifacts
Concept artwork was developed in the Packaging/Labels working conversation on 2026-09-03 using the existing family orchid artwork and the Selank label composition as the visual reference. The review set includes blue, green/teal, gold, purple and terracotta variants.

The current repository does not yet contain those binary concept images. Therefore this request records the proposed information architecture and exact elements requiring review, but does not pretend the artwork itself has been versioned or approved. Packaging should migrate the chosen review renders/source assets through the normal controlled asset process before any visual sign-off is treated as final.
