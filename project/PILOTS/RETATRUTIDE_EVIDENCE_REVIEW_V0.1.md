# Retatrutide Evidence Review v0.1

Status: EVIDENCE REVIEW WORKING RESULT / NOT PUBLICATION AUTHORIZATION
Evidence cutoff: 2026-09-03
Pilot: PB-SCI-PILOT-001

## Review purpose

Review the Retatrutide Evidence Inventory v0.1 and Claim Map v0.1 against currently verifiable primary human literature, clinical-trial registry records and official sponsor/regulatory-status material. This review determines which claim packages are sufficiently supported for editorial transformation and which remain limited, unresolved, unsupported or prohibited.

This file does not authorize clinical use, prescribing, self-administration or publication.

## Review outcome summary

- Core identity/mechanism: ACCEPTED WITH NORMAL MECHANISTIC LIMITS.
- Phase 1b pharmacokinetics: ACCEPTED WITH EARLY-PHASE LIMITS.
- Type 2 diabetes efficacy: ACCEPTED, including peer-reviewed phase 3 TRANSCEND-T2D-1.
- Obesity efficacy: ACCEPTED for phase 2; later phase 3 sponsor-reported obesity results remain NON-P1 until a peer-reviewed primary publication is accepted.
- MASLD liver-fat findings: ACCEPTED WITH SURROGATE-OUTCOME LIMITS.
- GI tolerability and observed heart-rate signal: ACCEPTED WITH TRIAL-SCOPE LIMITS.
- Long-term cardiovascular and renal event reduction: NOT ESTABLISHED.
- Superiority versus tirzepatide: NOT ESTABLISHED; head-to-head TRIUMPH-5 remains active/not recruiting at cutoff.
- Superiority versus semaglutide in T2D: NOT ESTABLISHED; TRANSCEND-T2D-2 remains active/not recruiting at cutoff.
- Approved clinical dosing: NOT APPLICABLE / NOT ESTABLISHED because retatrutide remains investigational and unapproved at cutoff.
- Third-party vial reconstitution, storage lifetime and stability: NOT VERIFIED; blocked from evidence-based publication instructions.
- Stacking/cycle protocols: NOT ESTABLISHED.

## Source-level review decisions

### E-001 — Phase 1b, T2D, Lancet 2022
Decision: ACCEPTED AS P1.

Accepted uses:
- identity as a triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist;
- once-weekly subcutaneous investigational administration;
- early PK characterization;
- approximately 6-day half-life as reported in early human testing;
- early glucose/body-weight pharmacodynamic signals;
- early tolerability description.

Mandatory limitations:
- 72 participants;
- 12-week duration;
- early-phase multiple-ascending-dose design;
- extensive sponsor involvement.

Not authorized from this source alone:
- long-term safety;
- approved dosing;
- general-population efficacy conclusions.

### E-002 — Phase 2 T2D, Lancet 2023
Decision: ACCEPTED AS P1.

Accepted uses:
- dose-dependent HbA1c reduction in the studied T2D population;
- substantial body-weight reduction over 36 weeks;
- comparison with placebo and dulaglutide 1.5 mg within the actual study design;
- GI adverse-event profile;
- absence of severe hypoglycaemia and deaths in this trial.

Mandatory limitations:
- phase 2;
- 36 weeks;
- comparator must be named exactly; findings must not be converted into superiority over current incretin therapy as a class;
- sponsor funding and relationships remain visible.

### E-003 — Phase 2 obesity, NEJM 2023
Decision: ACCEPTED AS P1.

Accepted uses:
- substantial dose-dependent weight reduction over 48 weeks in adults with obesity/overweight meeting trial criteria;
- mean 48-week weight changes reported for study arms, including -24.2% in the 12 mg arm versus -2.1% placebo;
- GI adverse events as the most frequent tolerability signal;
- observation that lower starting-dose strategies reduced GI burden in the trial;
- transient/dose-related heart-rate increase described in the report;
- observed pancreatitis and altered-skin-sensation signals as reported safety observations.

Mandatory limitations:
- trial regimen is not an approved prescription;
- phase 2 and finite duration;
- no claim of permanent weight loss after discontinuation;
- no claim of long-term safety or rare-event exclusion;
- no self-directed titration recommendation may be derived from the escalation comparison.

### E-004 — MASLD phase 2a substudy, Nature Medicine 2024
Decision: ACCEPTED AS P1 WITH SURROGATE LIMIT.

Accepted uses:
- substantial MRI-measured liver-fat reduction in the studied subgroup;
- dose-related proportions reaching liver fat below 5% at week 24.

Mandatory limitations:
- n=98 substudy;
- liver-fat imaging outcome is not equivalent to histologic fibrosis reversal, prevention of cirrhosis, liver-related event reduction or mortality benefit.

### E-005 — TRANSCEND-T2D-1, Lancet 2026
Decision: ACCEPTED AS P1 / STRONGEST CURRENT T2D EFFICACY EVIDENCE IN THIS REVIEW.

Accepted uses:
- significant HbA1c improvement versus placebo at 40 weeks across 4, 9 and 12 mg study arms;
- significant body-weight reduction in the studied monotherapy population;
- GI adverse-event profile;
- reported treatment discontinuations due to adverse events;
- no severe hypoglycaemia in the trial;
- two deaths in the 4 mg arm assessed as unrelated to study drug.

Mandatory limitations:
- 40-week trial;
- diet/exercise monotherapy population with defined entry criteria;
- cannot be automatically generalized to insulin-treated populations, severe renal impairment, pregnancy, paediatrics or long-term outcomes;
- sponsor funding/author relationships remain visible.

### E-006 — Structural/mechanistic evidence
Decision: ACCEPTED AS P2 FOR MECHANISM ONLY.

Accepted uses:
- support for direct interaction/activation architecture at GIPR, GLP-1R and GCGR;
- receptor-level mechanistic explanation.

Prohibited transformation:
- receptor pharmacology may not be converted into proof of human clinical efficacy, event reduction or long-term safety.

## Registry-level review decisions

### TRIUMPH-5 — NCT06662383
Decision: ACCEPTED AS R1, RESULTS NOT AVAILABLE FOR EFFICACY CONCLUSION AT CUTOFF.

At cutoff the registry describes an active, not recruiting phase 3 randomized double-blind comparison of retatrutide versus tirzepatide in adults with obesity, with estimated completion in December 2026.

Publication consequence:
- any claim that retatrutide is superior to tirzepatide remains UNRESOLVED.

### TRANSCEND-T2D-2 — NCT06260722
Decision: ACCEPTED AS R1, RESULTS NOT AVAILABLE FOR COMPARATIVE CONCLUSION AT CUTOFF.

At cutoff the registry describes an active, not recruiting phase 3 comparison with semaglutide in adults with T2D receiving metformin with or without an SGLT2 inhibitor.

Publication consequence:
- any claim of superior glycaemic efficacy versus semaglutide remains UNRESOLVED.

### TRANSCEND-T2D-3 — NCT06297603
Decision: ACCEPTED AS R1, RESULTS PENDING.

The study addresses T2D with moderate/severe renal impairment and basal-insulin background therapy.

Publication consequence:
- do not generalize monotherapy safety, hypoglycaemia or dosing observations to this population until results are reviewed.

### TRIUMPH-Outcomes — NCT06383390
Decision: ACCEPTED AS R1, NO RESULTS POSTED AT CUTOFF.

Publication consequence:
- cardiovascular-event and kidney-outcome benefit remain UNRESOLVED.
- improvement in surrogate cardiometabolic markers must not be presented as demonstrated event reduction.

## Regulatory-status review

Publication consequence:

## Evidence-strength interpretation for PeptiBloom

### Solid evidence

- Triple receptor pharmacology as the intended molecular mechanism.
- Clinically meaningful glycaemic lowering in adults with T2D in studied populations, including one peer-reviewed phase 3 trial.
- Substantial weight reduction in phase 2 obesity research and in T2D studies.
- GI adverse effects as a recurrent common tolerability signal in the human trials reviewed.

### Limited but meaningful evidence

- Approximately 6-day half-life from early-phase human testing.
- Liver-fat reduction in MASLD based on a small imaging substudy.
- Heart-rate and altered-sensation safety signals requiring appropriate context.
- Sponsor-reported phase 3 obesity outcomes that have not yet been upgraded to peer-reviewed full primary evidence in this review.

### Unresolved

- Long-term cardiovascular benefit.
- Hard renal outcomes.
- Long-term rare-event safety.
- Comparative superiority versus tirzepatide or semaglutide.
- Pregnancy/lactation, paediatric use and several special-population questions.
- Evidence-based third-party vial reconstitution/stability.

### Anecdotal

No anecdotal/community evidence is accepted into Evidence Review v0.1. Community experience can be studied in a later, separately labeled layer but cannot alter scientific claim status.
