## Required corrections before editorial review

### R-001 — Formula and molecular weight wording

The draft currently presents C221H342N46O68 and approximately 4,731.36 Da as the fully modified neutral-molecule representation based on FDA GSRS alternative chemical information.

Decision: RETAIN ONLY WITH EXPLICIT REPRESENTATION QUALIFIER.

Reason: peptide/substance databases can expose backbone/protein calculations separately from chemically modified substance representations. Public copy must identify which representation is being reported and must not imply that a vendor salt/form or every material labelled retatrutide has exactly the same reported mass.

Required wording principle: “FDA GSRS alternative chemical representation for the fully modified neutral molecule” rather than an unqualified universal formula/MW statement.

### R-002 — Sequence representation

The 39-residue one-letter sequence may be retained only as a backbone representation. It must remain adjacent to the warning that non-standard residues, lipidation/linker chemistry and terminal modification are not fully encoded by the natural-amino-acid one-letter string.

Decision: PASS WITH QUALIFIER.

### R-003 — Regulatory status

Retain “investigational / not approved at cutoff” with a dated source. Do not convert developer statements about intended future submissions into approval expectations or timelines in the core scientific conclusion.

Decision: PASS.

### R-004 — Dose and frequency

All numerical dose values and once-weekly language must remain explicitly described as investigational trial regimens/study administration.

No wording may imply an approved dose, recommended maintenance dose, self-directed titration or PeptiBloom protocol.

Decision: PASS WITH HARD EDITORIAL GUARDRAIL.

### R-005 — Safety wording

The draft correctly reports recurrent GI adverse events, the phase-2 heart-rate observation, one serious pancreatitis event in the obesity trial, pancreatic-enzyme observations and altered/enhanced skin sensation.

Required preservation:
- do not infer incidence outside the cited trial;
- do not infer class-wide contraindications from other incretin products;
- do not describe absence of severe hypoglycaemia in selected trials as proof of absence under insulin or other combination therapy;
- long-term rare-event safety remains incomplete.

Decision: PASS WITH LIMITS.

### R-006 — MASLD wording

MRI-measured liver-fat reduction is supported. Any language implying fibrosis reversal, prevention of cirrhosis, reduced liver-related mortality or established disease modification is prohibited by the current Evidence Review.

Decision: PASS.

### R-007 — Comparative claims

Retatrutide superiority versus tirzepatide and versus semaglutide remains unresolved at the cutoff. Registry existence does not supply comparative efficacy results.

Decision: PASS; retain explicit unresolved wording.

### R-008 — Cardiovascular and renal outcomes

Improvements in weight or cardiometabolic risk markers must not be transformed into proven cardiovascular-event or hard renal-outcome benefit. TRIUMPH-Outcomes is evidence of an ongoing question, not evidence of benefit.

Decision: PASS.

### R-009 — Reconstitution, stability and storage

The draft correctly refuses to create universal third-party-vial instructions. No bacteriostatic-water volume, post-reconstitution lifetime, refrigerator duration, syringe-unit conversion or vendor-derived generic storage rule may be added during editorial polishing.

Decision: PASS / BLOCK REMAINS.

### R-010 — Contraindications and monitoring

Because there is no approved prescribing label at cutoff, the draft correctly avoids inventing an official contraindication list or a PeptiBloom clinical-monitoring protocol.

Decision: PASS.

## Numerical QC

Verified against the accepted Evidence Review/inventory:

- Phase 2 obesity, 48 weeks: mean body-weight change in 12 mg arm = -24.2%; placebo = -2.1%.
- Phase 2 T2D, 36 weeks: highest studied dose group approximately -16.9% body weight; placebo -3.0%; dulaglutide -2.0% as recorded in the Evidence Inventory.
- TRANSCEND-T2D-1, 40 weeks: HbA1c changes recorded as -1.69%, -1.86%, -1.94% for 4/9/12 mg versus -0.81% placebo; body-weight changes -11.5%, -13.9%, -15.3% versus -2.6% placebo under the stated estimand.
- MASLD substudy, 24 weeks: relative liver-fat changes approximately -42.9%, -57.0%, -81.4%, -82.4% for 1/4/8/12 mg versus +0.3% placebo; proportions below 5% liver fat 27%, 52%, 79%, 86% versus 0% placebo.
- Early human half-life: approximately 6 days.

No mg↔mcg or insulin-unit calculations are present in the public draft; therefore no such conversion is authorized or required for this recheck.

Accepted claims were not materially strengthened in the draft.

The following boundaries remain intact:

- mechanism != proof of each clinical effect;
- trial regimen != approved dose;
- trial duration != recommended cycle;
- registry question != positive outcome;
- liver-fat reduction != fibrosis reversal;

## Recommendation PeptiBloom recheck

Authorized conclusion shape:

Retatrutide can be described as an investigational triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist with strong short-to-medium-term human evidence for glycaemic improvement in studied T2D populations and substantial weight reduction in studied populations, while long-term outcomes, rare-event safety, direct comparative superiority, approved dosing and third-party preparation/stability remain unresolved or unavailable.

- a recommended cycle;
- a reconstitution schedule;
- a syringe-unit conversion;
- a stacking protocol;
- a claim that the product is proven safe long term;
- a claim that phase 3 comparative superiority has been established where results remain pending.
