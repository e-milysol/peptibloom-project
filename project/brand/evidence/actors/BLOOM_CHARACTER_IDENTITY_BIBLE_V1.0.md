## Canonical identity

Bloom is PeptiBloom's Communicator & Educator / Ambassador. She connects science with people through clarity, empathy and curiosity, introduces topics, asks accessible questions, translates complexity without erasing uncertainty, and keeps the audience engaged.

Personality anchors: warm, curious, positive, approachable, inspiring, people-oriented and encouraging rather than authoritative. The approved master contains the character-brand catchphrase `Curiosity blooms knowledge.`; it is not a scientific claim.

## Visual invariants

Bloom remains a stylized young-adult female character, apparent late 20s–early 30s, with the same recognizable facial proportions as the canonical master; medium-light/warm skin tone as represented there; large expressive brown eyes; soft arched brows; refined nose; friendly facial structure; natural makeup/soft lip treatment; long voluminous dark-brown wavy hair with side part; and consistent slim-to-average stylized body proportions.

Canonical default wardrobe: white laboratory coat with purple-family accents, purple/violet inner top, dark fitted trousers, white sneakers with subtle purple detailing, and orchid motif/pin. Her visual medium remains polished stylized character illustration rather than photoreal human portraiture.

Do not under v1.0 materially change face, eye color, apparent age, hair color/length/silhouette, body proportions, default wardrobe identity, signature orchid identity, or visual medium in a way that makes her a different character. No silent ethnicity-coded drift, permanent glasses, exaggerated glamour, anime/chibi recast, photoreal recast, sexualization, childlike proportions, or random wardrobe replacement.

Natural secondary hair motion, head-angle changes, minor strand variation, coat movement, blinks, eyelines and production derivatives are allowed when identity is preserved.

## Color and signature system

Bloom's signature family is **Bloom Orchid Purple**. The source sheet visually presents purple/lilac primary swatches and neutral charcoal-to-white supporting tones. Any tiny AI-rendered hex text visible inside the sheet is reference-only until independently sampled/verified; the exact binary is the visual authority. Bloom must not be arbitrarily converted to Neuro blue, Lab teal, Metabolic gold or Blend terracotta.

Signature visual language includes orchid and orchid + DNA/molecular imagery. Supporting props may include tablet, notebook, research vial in scientific-context scenes, pen, orchid pin and PeptiBloom ID badge. Props are context, not evidence, and generated label/product text must not be invented casually.

## Expression vocabulary

The approved master establishes the initial controlled vocabulary: neutral, happy, curious, surprised, excited, thoughtful, explaining, concerned, focused, confident, empathetic, encouraging, questioning, playful and grateful.

Expressions must preserve facial identity and must not visually upgrade scientific certainty or risk. Avoid grotesque distortion, sexualization, theatrical fear or celebratory efficacy cues. For uncertainty, prefer curious, thoughtful, focused, empathetic or concerned treatments.

## Pose and gesture vocabulary

The master supports presenting a topic, explaining an idea, holding a vial in scientific context, confident standing, welcoming/introducing and interaction with Eviden. New derivatives should preserve open audience-friendly body language, professional scientific/editorial context, natural eyelines and coherent hand gestures. Avoid aggressive/dismissive gestures, influencer-style theatrical posing, implied clinical authority, or random body-shape drift.

## Voice/personality direction

The source master describes Bloom's voice direction as female, friendly, clear, professional and warm. She speaks clearly and simply, asks questions and encourages learning. She is curious, connects dots, sees the big picture and remains people-oriented. She avoids overpromising, jargon-first communication, unnecessary technicality and assumptions.

This is character direction only. No synthetic voice is canonical yet; reproducible voice definition belongs to AV-08 through AV-10.

## Editorial behavior

Bloom may introduce questions, request clarification, summarize approved explanations in accessible language, connect sections, express curiosity and use approved analogies. She must not invent scientific claims, upgrade evidence strength, imply personal medical expertise, convert uncertainty into certainty, state unsupported efficacy/safety claims, or use laboratory props as proof.

With Eviden, Bloom frames audience questions, requests explanation, translates/restates without changing evidence strength, guides transitions and supplies accessibility/warmth. Eviden supplies analytical qualification. Exact duo scale, spacing, eyelines and blocking remain deferred to AV-06.

## Allowed controlled derivatives

Allowed when traceable to this master: expressions consistent with the canonical face; gestures/poses consistent with canonical proportions; transparent cutouts; rigged/segmented assets; lip-sync mouth shapes; blink/eye-direction variants; camera-angle derivatives; approved prop handling; 2D/2.5D animation derivatives; talking-avatar profiles; and stills for articles, infographics, social and video.

Material changes to permanent hairstyle, eye color, face, apparent age, default wardrobe, signature identifiers, body proportions, core palette/role or a visual-medium transition that changes identity require a new approved character version and must not be called Bloom v1.0.

## Drift QA

Every derivative must be checked for recognizable canonical face; long dark-brown wavy side-parted hair; brown eyes; consistent apparent age and body proportions; correct wardrobe/color identity when applicable; correct orchid elements; coherent anatomy; absence of hallucinated logos/text; absence of unsupported scientific implication from props/pose; appropriate expression strength; and traceability to `PB-EV-BLOOM-CHARACTER-MASTER-V1.0`.

`CHECKSUM_MISMATCH = NOT_CANONICAL` applies to the master binary. Frozen identity rules must not be silently overwritten.

## Deferred technical decisions

This bible does not define rig bones, viseme set, numerical body measurements, animation frame rate, voice provider/model, pronunciation lexicon, camera-angle limits, duo scale metrics, final lighting system or commercial avatar platform. Those belong to later AV checkpoints.
