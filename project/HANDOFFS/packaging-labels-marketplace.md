## CURRENT_STATE

Packaging / Labels / Marketplace Visuals has operational ownership for packaging and label information architecture, applied-brand layout, production-artwork state tracking, and marketplace-visual preparation within approved upstream inputs and downstream gates.

It coordinates authoritative inputs; it does not become authority for those inputs.

Current packaging/label designs, NIIMBOT concepts, holographic/security treatments and marketplace concepts recovered from legacy material remain reconciliation evidence unless separately approved. No recovered screenshot or mockup is production-approved artwork. No marketplace eligibility or READY_FOR_SALE state is implied.

## WORKSTREAM AUTHORITY

This workstream owns:

- packaging and label information architecture;
- separation and presentation of fixed versus variable/product/batch-specific information;
- applied-brand layout using approved Brand assets/rules;
- layout specifications consuming authoritative product identity, physical constraints, legal copy and scientific content;
- artwork lifecycle/status distinction between design specification, print test and production-approved artwork;
- marketplace visual asset preparation after applicable product/platform/legal gates are satisfied;
- reconciliation/statusing of legacy packaging, label, holographic and marketplace visual concepts;
- sanitized handoffs of approved packaging/visual assets or specifications to downstream consumers.

## WORKSTREAM NON-AUTHORITY

This workstream does not own or authorize:

- canonical Brand identity or logo creation;
- physical product identity, geometry, dimensions, materials, fit/function or physical validation;
- Catalog/SKU/product/pricing truth;
- scientific claims, evidence levels, efficacy, safety or scientific interpretation;
- mandatory legal/regulatory label content, product classification, warnings, jurisdictional obligations or legal clearance;
- private supplier/procurement/inventory/landed-cost truth;
- Web implementation/publication;
- Commerce eligibility, payment authorization, marketplace eligibility, listing publication or READY_FOR_SALE;
- Growth strategy or moderation-evasion tactics.

## AUTHORITY INTERFACES

- Brand / Visual: consumes the canonical master `apps/web/public/brand/logo.png` and approved derivatives/rules. The master is OFFICIAL / MASTER / DO NOT REGENERATE. Decorative color must not be assigned scientific evidence meaning.
- Physical Products / Accessories: consumes authoritative dimensions, label/surface constraints, material compatibility and packaging-envelope facts when evidenced. Packaging does not invent dimensions or geometry.
- Catalog / Pricing: consumes authoritative product/SKU/variant/pricing/public-status facts. `PRIVATE COST != PUBLIC PRICE`; catalog inclusion does not imply publication, commercial eligibility or READY_FOR_SALE.
- Legal / Regulatory: supplies applicable mandatory legal/regulatory requirements and approved legal copy. Unresolved legal input makes affected output BLOCKED or PLACEHOLDER-ONLY; Packaging does not fabricate legal copy.
- Evidence / Scientific Content: supplies approved scientific truth/claims. Packaging must not create, strengthen or visually manufacture evidence claims. `VISUAL COLOR != SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE GRADE`.
- Commerce / Web / Growth: downstream consumers/requirement sources only within their authority; their request does not itself authorize Packaging content or publication.
- WORK 7 / Private Master: private supplier, acquisition price, landed cost, payment, tracking, inventory, procurement documents and restricted operational codes remain private. Public handoffs are sanitized only.

## LABEL INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Recovered durable direction supports separating:

1. FIXED INFORMATION — stable packaging/applied-brand information appropriate to a reusable/generic structure where later approved; and
2. VARIABLE / BATCH / PRODUCT-SPECIFIC INFORMATION — product/batch facts supplied by authoritative upstream sources.

Historical examples include product identity, lot, date, concentration and QR-linked metadata. These are architectural examples only and are not declarations of legally mandatory fields.

Any QR or machine-readable code must have a real maintained purpose/destination before production use; decorative/dead codes are not approved.

## NIIMBOT / PRINTING BOUNDARY

Keep these states distinct:

- DESIGN_SPECIFICATION;
- PRINT_TEST;
- PRODUCTION_APPROVED_ARTWORK.

A successful printer output proves only the print test performed; it does not prove legal correctness, physical suitability, regulatory compliance or production approval. Historical NIIMBOT observations/templates are implementation evidence only until verified against current hardware/supplies and approved content.
