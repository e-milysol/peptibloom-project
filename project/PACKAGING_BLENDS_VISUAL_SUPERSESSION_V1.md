## New visual direction — frozen

Blends Series now uses a **warm neutral luxury-science palette** rather than the earlier orange-forward terracotta/coral treatment.

The frozen direction is qualitative until a clean controlled color-spec sheet is generated:
- deep chocolate / espresso brown for family bands and high-contrast anchors;
- taupe / warm brown for mid tones;
- champagne / bronze metallic notes for molecules and DNA;
- cream / ivory / beige for the orchid and light fields;
- restrained warm-gold highlights are permitted as accents;
- **avoid dominant orange / coral / saturated terracotta appearance**.

No Pantone or exact HEX values are promoted by this supersession document. Numeric palette authority must come from a new clean Blends color-spec sheet built from the approved warm-neutral direction and explicitly reviewed.

## Artwork behavior — frozen

The approved Blends pilot keeps the shared family-card language:
