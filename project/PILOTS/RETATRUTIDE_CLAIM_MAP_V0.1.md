# Retatrutide Claim Map v0.1

Status: WORKING CLAIM MAP / NOT PUBLICATION-READY
Evidence cutoff: 2026-09-03
Pilot: PB-SCI-PILOT-001

## Purpose

Map candidate public claims to the strongest evidence currently identified. This file is deliberately conservative: unsupported or immature claims remain blocked rather than being filled from vendor practice, inference or anecdote.

## Claim status vocabulary

- SUPPORTED — directly supported by adequate identified evidence for the stated scope.
- SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS — supported, but population/duration/design limits must accompany the claim.
- EMERGING — meaningful signal exists but evidence is incomplete for a strong public conclusion.
- UNRESOLVED — relevant studies are ongoing or evidence is insufficient/contradictory.
- UNSUPPORTED — no adequate evidence identified for the proposed claim.
- PROHIBITED_TRANSFORMATION — source exists, but converting it into the proposed public claim would overstate the evidence.

## Identity and mechanism

### C-001
Claim: Retatrutide (LY3437943) is a single peptide agonist of GIPR, GLP-1R and GCGR.
Status: SUPPORTED.
Evidence: E-001, E-002, E-005, E-006.
Publication note: preferred scientific descriptor is triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist. Informal labels such as 'GLP-3' are not scientific classifications.

### C-002
Claim: Triple receptor engagement is intended to combine incretin effects with glucagon-receptor-mediated metabolic effects.
Status: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS.
Evidence: E-006 plus preclinical pharmacology cited in peer-reviewed clinical papers.
Boundary: mechanistic rationale must not be worded as proof that GCGR activation alone causes the magnitude of human weight loss.

### C-003
Claim: Retatrutide increases energy expenditure in humans via glucagon receptor agonism.
Status: UNRESOLVED.
Reason: preclinical evidence supports GCGR-dependent energy-expenditure effects, but a direct quantitative human causal claim is not established by the core human trials reviewed here.

## Pharmacokinetics and frequency

### C-010
Claim: Retatrutide has an approximately 6-day half-life in early human clinical testing.
Status: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS.
Evidence: E-001.
Boundary: derived from phase 1b; later population PK analysis may refine the value.

### C-011
Claim: The investigational clinical programme uses once-weekly subcutaneous administration.
Status: SUPPORTED.
Evidence: E-001 through E-005 and phase 3 registry records.
Boundary: this describes study administration, not an approved prescribing instruction.

## Glycaemic efficacy

### C-020
Claim: Retatrutide lowers HbA1c in adults with type 2 diabetes.
Status: SUPPORTED.
Evidence: E-001, E-002, E-005.
Strongest current evidence: peer-reviewed phase 3 TRANSCEND-T2D-1.

### C-021
Claim: In TRANSCEND-T2D-1, 40 weeks of retatrutide produced greater HbA1c reductions than placebo across 4, 9 and 12 mg study arms.
Status: SUPPORTED.
Evidence: E-005.
Scope: adults with T2D inadequately controlled by diet and exercise alone.

### C-022
Claim: Retatrutide is superior to semaglutide for glycaemic control.
Status: UNRESOLVED.
Reason: TRANSCEND-T2D-2 directly compares the drugs but was not completed with peer-reviewed results at cutoff.

## Body-weight efficacy

### C-030
Claim: Retatrutide produces substantial dose-dependent body-weight reduction in adults with obesity without diabetes over 48 weeks.
Status: SUPPORTED.
Evidence: E-003.
Anchor result: mean -24.2% at 48 weeks in the 12 mg phase 2 arm versus -2.1% placebo.

### C-031
Claim: Retatrutide produces substantial body-weight reduction in adults with type 2 diabetes.
Status: SUPPORTED.
Evidence: E-002 and E-005.
Boundary: magnitude varies by population, dose, duration and estimand.

### C-032
Claim: Retatrutide causes approximately 28% mean weight loss at 80 weeks in obesity.
Status: EMERGING.
Evidence: C-001 sponsor-reported TRIUMPH-1 data.
Boundary: may only be presented as sponsor-reported/topline or conference-presented until a full peer-reviewed primary publication is accepted into Evidence.

### C-033
Claim: Retatrutide is more effective than tirzepatide for obesity.
Status: UNRESOLVED.
Reason: TRIUMPH-5 head-to-head trial ongoing at cutoff.

### C-034
Claim: Weight loss is permanent after discontinuation.
Status: UNSUPPORTED.
Reason: no adequate long-term randomized withdrawal evidence identified in this v0.1 map.

## MASLD / liver outcomes

### C-040
Claim: Retatrutide substantially reduces MRI-measured liver fat in participants with MASLD and obesity.
Status: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS.
Evidence: E-004.
Scope: small phase 2a substudy; strongest 24-week liver-fat reductions occurred at higher doses.

### C-041
Claim: Retatrutide reverses liver fibrosis or prevents cirrhosis.
Status: UNSUPPORTED.
Reason: liver-fat reduction is not equivalent to proven fibrosis regression or hard liver-outcome benefit.

## Cardiovascular and renal outcomes

### C-050
Claim: Retatrutide prevents cardiovascular events.
Status: UNRESOLVED.
Reason: TRIUMPH-Outcomes remains ongoing/active at cutoff.

### C-051
Claim: Retatrutide improves hard kidney outcomes.
Status: UNRESOLVED.
Reason: dedicated outcome evidence is not complete.

### C-052
Claim: Retatrutide lowers cardiometabolic risk markers such as body weight, triglycerides and blood pressure-related measures.
Status: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS.
Evidence: phase 2/phase 3 trials report improvements in several risk markers.
Boundary: surrogate/risk-marker improvement must not be transformed into proven event reduction.

## Safety and tolerability

### C-060
Claim: Gastrointestinal adverse events are the most common adverse effects identified in clinical trials.
Status: SUPPORTED.
Evidence: E-001, E-002, E-003, E-005.

### C-061
Claim: GI adverse effects are dose/escalation related and slower/lower starting escalation can improve tolerability.
Status: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS.
Evidence: E-003; phase 2 design directly compared starting-dose approaches.
Boundary: describe the trial observation; do not convert it into a self-directed titration recommendation.

### C-062
Claim: Retatrutide can increase heart rate.
Status: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS.
Evidence: E-003; dose-dependent increase peaking around week 24 and declining thereafter.

### C-063
Claim: Retatrutide has no pancreatitis risk.
Status: UNSUPPORTED.
Reason: phase 2 obesity trial reported one serious acute pancreatitis event; sample sizes are inadequate to rule out uncommon risks.

### C-064
Claim: Retatrutide is proven safe long term.
Status: UNSUPPORTED.
Reason: investigational status, finite trial durations and incomplete rare-event characterization.

### C-065
Claim: Severe hypoglycaemia is common with retatrutide monotherapy.
Status: UNSUPPORTED by the reviewed monotherapy/phase 2 evidence; severe hypoglycaemia was not reported in E-002 or E-005.
Boundary: this cannot be generalized to all future combination regimens, especially insulin-containing regimens.

### C-066
Claim: Altered skin sensation/hyperesthesia can occur.
Status: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS.
Evidence: E-003; reported in a minority and not severe/serious in that trial.

## Regulatory and availability claims

### C-070
Claim: Retatrutide is FDA-approved.
Status: UNSUPPORTED / FALSE at cutoff.
Evidence: official Lilly status, July 2026; investigational and not approved by any regulatory agency at cutoff.

### C-071
Claim: A standard approved dose exists.
Status: UNSUPPORTED / FALSE at cutoff.
Reason: no regulatory approval; doses in publications are trial regimens.

## Preparation, storage and reconstitution

### C-080
Claim: PeptiBloom can recommend an evidence-based bacteriostatic-water reconstitution protocol for third-party retatrutide vials.
Status: UNSUPPORTED.
Reason: no approved commercial retatrutide product/reconstitution instructions and no validated third-party-vial evidence identified.
Publication rule: blocked.

### C-081
Claim: A reconstituted third-party retatrutide vial is stable for a specific number of days refrigerated.
Status: UNSUPPORTED in v0.1.
Publication rule: no stability duration may be inferred from vendor instructions, community practice or unrelated peptide products.

### C-082
Claim: Trial-supply storage conditions can be transferred directly to aftermarket lyophilised material.
Status: PROHIBITED_TRANSFORMATION.
Reason: formulation, container closure, excipients, manufacturing controls and analytical stability may differ.

## Combinations / stacking

### C-090
Claim: Retatrutide should be stacked with another GLP-1/GIP agent for greater weight loss.
Status: UNSUPPORTED.
Reason: no evidence-based clinical stacking protocol identified; overlapping pharmacology raises safety/interpretability concerns.

### C-091
Claim: Retatrutide plus insulin has an established general safety/dosing protocol.
Status: UNRESOLVED.
Reason: dedicated TRANSCEND-T2D-3 study in renal impairment/on basal insulin is ongoing; no generalized conclusion should be inferred.

## Pregnancy, paediatrics and special populations

Claim: Retatrutide is established as safe in pregnancy or lactation.
Status: UNSUPPORTED.

### C-101
Claim: Retatrutide is established as safe/effective in children or adolescents.
Status: UNSUPPORTED in this v0.1 adult-focused evidence map.

### C-102
Claim: Retatrutide is established as safe/effective in severe renal impairment.
Status: UNRESOLVED pending dedicated phase 3 evidence.

## Cancer claims

### C-110
Claim: Retatrutide treats or prevents human cancer.
Status: PROHIBITED_TRANSFORMATION.
Reason: peer-reviewed preclinical mouse/cancer-model findings exist, but no human anti-cancer efficacy claim is established.

## PeptiBloom recommendation layer

### C-120
Candidate public conclusion: Current evidence supports describing retatrutide as a highly active investigational triple-receptor agonist with robust short-to-medium-term effects on body weight and glycaemic control, but incomplete long-term safety, cardiovascular/renal outcome and regulatory evidence.
Status: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS.
Required qualifiers:
- investigational/not approved at cutoff;
- separate peer-reviewed phase 3 evidence from sponsor topline obesity results;
- no approved dose/reconstitution protocol;
- no extrapolation to self-directed use;
- unresolved long-term hard outcomes.

### C-121
Candidate PeptiBloom recommendation: PeptiBloom recommends a specific retatrutide dose, cycle or reconstitution schedule for public use.
Status: BLOCKED / NOT EVIDENCE-AUTHORIZED.
Reason: investigational drug, no approved prescribing information, and the current Evidence corpus does not justify transforming trial regimens or third-party practices into public instructions.

## Editorial priority for first article

Safe-to-draft after Evidence acceptance:
1. What retatrutide is and why triple agonism matters.
2. Development history and current investigational status.
3. Human efficacy evidence separated by phase/population.
4. MASLD signal with explicit surrogate-outcome limitation.
5. Known tolerability/safety signals and unknowns.
6. Active phase 3 programme and what questions it is designed to answer.
7. What is not known: long-term outcomes, comparative superiority, rare safety, pregnancy/paediatrics, approved dosing, third-party stability/reconstitution.
8. Evidence-level summary and complete references.

Blocked from public instructional wording:
- self-administration dose;
- titration recommendation;
- 'cycle' duration;
- bacteriostatic-water reconstitution instructions;
- insulin-syringe unit conversion for retatrutide use;
- third-party vial storage lifetime;
- stacking protocols.
